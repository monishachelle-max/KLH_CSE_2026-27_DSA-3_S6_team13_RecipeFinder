
Recipe: Cake
Ingredients:
Flour - 2 cups
Sugar - 1 cup
Eggs - 2
Milk - 1 cup
Butter - 100 grams
Baking Powder - 1 teaspoon

Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 180 degrees Celsius.
2. Mix flour, sugar and baking powder in a bowl.
3. Add eggs, milk and melted butter.
4. Mix all the ingredients until the batter becomes smooth.
5. Pour the batter into a greased baking pan.
6. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes.
7. Allow the cake to cool before serving.

Preparation Time: 20 minutes
Cooking Time: 35 minutes

Recipe: Masala Dosa

Onion - 2
Green Chilli - 2
Mustard Seeds - 1 teaspoon
Oil - 3 tablespoons
Salt - 1 teaspoon

Instructions:

7. Add the potato filling and fold the dosa.
8. Serve hot with chutney and sambar.

Turmeric Powder - 1/2 teaspoon
Garam Masala - 1 teaspoon

Salt - 1 teaspoon

Instructions:
